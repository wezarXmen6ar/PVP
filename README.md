# PRMS — Portfolio & Resource Management System

## Mission

**Make the cost of every project decision visible to the person making it.**

The General Department of Artificial Intelligence delivers projects for the whole of Dubai Police from one finite pool of people. Demand comes from every direction — HQ directives, departmental requests, new requirements mid-delivery — but no one requesting work can see what it costs the portfolio, because nothing makes that cost visible at the moment of the request.

PRMS exists to close that gap. It holds the department's people and projects in one live model, so that:

- a new requirement is answered with the man-days it adds and the date it moves, before it is accepted;
- halting a project for higher-priority work shows what it does to the project being halted, before the decision is taken;
- every delivery date that moves has a recorded cause attached to it.

The goal is not to say no. It is to make sure that when someone says yes, they know what they are saying yes to.

## Bio

I am a **Technical Project Manager in the General Department of Artificial Intelligence at Dubai Police.**

GD of AI is the central delivery department for digitalisation across the force. The other general departments — CID, Police Stations, Anti-Narcotics, Forensics, Prisons and the rest — own the business problems. We supply the Business Analysts, Developers, Tech Leads and Technical Project Managers who turn those problems into working systems.

When a department brings a project to us, I am assigned as its Technical PM. I assemble the delivery team, run the project from business analysis through to launch, and own the plan and timeline that get presented to the business owner and to HQ.

That role puts me between two groups who cannot see each other's constraints: business owners who do not know what their requests cost, and HQ leadership who reprioritise without sight of what it displaces. I absorb the difference, and I have no instrument for showing it.

**PRMS is that instrument.** I am building it because I am the one who needs it.
